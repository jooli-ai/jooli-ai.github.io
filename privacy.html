Hermes Calendar: privacy policy

Hermes Calendar is a private tool used by one family (see the home page). This policy says what Google data it uses and what happens to it.

What Google data it accesses

It asks Google for one permission only: to create secondary calendars and to see, create, change and delete events on the calendars it created. It uses that permission for one calendar, "Family", in its own Google account. On that calendar it reads events (title, date and time, place, description and guests) and creates events with family members as guests.

It does not access any other calendar, Gmail, contacts, files or other Google data.

How the data is used

Events are read only to answer a family member's question about the calendar, and created only when a family member asks. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is kept

Events stay in Google Calendar: on the Family calendar, and, as invitations, on the calendars of the family members invited. The tool keeps no copy of them. Its sign-in credentials are kept on one private computer, readable only by the family member who runs it. Its activity log records only the time, the action and whether it worked, never the content of an event.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with anyone outside the family, or sent to any AI model hosted outside that private computer. Hermes Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's settings (Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services"). The family members invited can delete an event from their own calendar at any time.
