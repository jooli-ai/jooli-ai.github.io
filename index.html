Hermes Calendar

Hermes Calendar is a private tool that keeps one family's shared calendar. It is not a public service, and nobody outside that family can sign up or use it.

What it does:

  • It creates one calendar, called "Family", in its own Google account, and works only on that calendar.
  • When a family member asks, it lists one person's events on that calendar for a day or a few days, or adds an event and invites the family members it concerns.
  • It can invite only the family members on a fixed list. It cannot read any other calendar, Gmail or other Google data.
